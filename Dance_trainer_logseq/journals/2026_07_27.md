- [decision] Labels are one shared `LabelBar`/`LabelPicker` across the card modal and the practice surfaces, themed by a `--lp-*` host contract rather than by props — the same mechanism `MarkdownNote` uses, and the second time it has paid for itself. Rejected building a practice-only picker beside the modal's existing inline adder: that is exactly the "CardModal gets the fix and the other surfaces never do" pattern the shared-component move was meant to end — [[Board View]]
- [decision] The label panel lists **every** label with a checkbox instead of only the addable ones. Rejected keeping the modal's add-only popover plus a chip-sized `×` for removal: two controls of different shapes for one job, and the `×` is unusable mid-practice — [[Board View]]
- [decision] The Board/Practice toggle became a single `role="switch"` button. Rejected keeping the `role="tablist"` pair and merely widening the hit areas: the complaint was that tapping the *lit* half did nothing, which no amount of padding fixes, and a two-state control was never a tablist — [[Board View]]
- [decision] The app shell clips instead of measuring: `html`/`body`/`#app` are `overflow: hidden` and every view scrolls inside itself. Rejected a third round of viewport-unit tuning — Chrome for Android fixes the initial containing block to the large viewport, so no `dvh` value on any descendant can stop the document scrolling by the URL bar's height, which is why two prior fixes narrowed the symptom without ending it. Cost: any new full-height view must declare its own scroller or its content is clipped — [[Board View]]
