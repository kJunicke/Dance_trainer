- **Nothing here is implemented.** Order below is the agreed working order: small bugs first, then flow, then access, then the doc/code divergence.
- ## Reading the score
	- 17/40 lands in the rubric's "Poor" band, which overstates it. Heuristics 6 (recognition), 9 (error recovery) and 10 (help) are scored against a general-audience bar, and a single-expert-user tool legitimately underinvests there.
	- The damage that actually reaches this user daily sits in **1 (system status)**, **3 (control/undo)** and **9 (error recovery)**: no confirmation that a session decision landed, no way to take it back, and error toasts that need Postgres literacy to parse.
	- The deterministic slop detector returned **zero findings** across ~5,100 lines of hand-written SFCs, verified against a synthetic control file. The codebase does not reach for template reflexes; the gap is information design, not craft.

	- TODO `.field-label` in `CardModal.vue:455` is a 12px mono uppercase tracked ember eyebrow, four stacked down one dialog — the labels shout and the values whisper. Flagged as an AI-grammar tell; here it's more simply a hierarchy inversion.

- ## Tier 7 — Worth deciding, not yet scheduled
	- Empty columns render as `0` + `+ Add Card`. *"Nothing due today"* is arguably the single most valuable message this app can display, and it currently shows as blank space.
	- The Practice surfaces have no desktop treatment — `position: fixed; inset: 0` with top-left content, so at 1440px they're a full-viewport black rectangle with a 40px badge in the corner. Either constrain to a centred column above ~700px, or hide the mode toggle on desktop.
	- The Done receipt fires as a 200ms background swap on the FAB (`PracticeView.vue:47`) — the element the thumb is physically covering at that instant. [[Practice Companion View]] records this as deliberate; the reasoning is right about *what* to reward and wrong about *where* to put it. The haptic survives; the visual is invisible by construction.
	- The `.done-fab` floats over note text with no reserved gutter — the last ~48px of every note sits permanently under an opaque orange pill.
	- `.grip` in the session list is `--pc-border` on `--pc-surface` = **1.51:1**, and it isn't actually the drag handle (the whole row is long-pressable). It signals something false while being unreadable.
	- Two identical 7px dots sit on one session row: `.row-dot` (urgency) at the left, `.label-dot` (label colour) at the right. A red "Drill" label dot is pixel-identical to an overdue status dot — directly against the "don't rely on colour alone" note in `PRODUCT.md`.
	- The board topbar permanently displays a raw invite UUID plus Copy/Export/Import — collaboration chrome eating prime space on a single-user tool, every day.
	- Toasts carry raw Supabase error strings, with no recovery action and no dismiss, for 4s. There is no success toast anywhere in the app.
	- All contextual help lives in `title=` attributes, which do nothing on the touch device this is built for.
- ## Coverage gaps in the critique itself
	- `resize_window` doesn't work in this environment (the tiling WM ignores it), so all mobile measurement ran inside a same-origin 390px iframe. Media queries and layout evaluate faithfully — but **real touch behaviour was never verified**: long-press-to-drag, `navigator.vibrate` haptics, `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding, iOS Safari's 16px zoom guard, and the PWA install/resume path are all unreviewed.
	- No Delete control was clicked anywhere, because native `window.confirm()` freezes the CDP connection for 30s+ per call. No destructive-flow copy or confirm wording was reviewed. Worth a manual pass — deleting a board is the app's most destructive action and has no undo and no type-the-name step.
